## 🎙️ Welcome to the Community

The CodeTrace Discord is a thriving server built for developers, engineers, and computer science students. Whether you are stuck on a LeetCode Hard, trying to understand a tricky asynchronous closure, or want to contribute to the CodeTrace open-source engine, our community is here 24/7.

> *"Code is written in isolation, but software is built in a community."*

---

## 🚀 Server Channels & Structure

Our server is organized into clear, focused categories so you can always find the right conversation.

### 📚 Learning & Help
- **`#general-chat`**: Hang out, introduce yourself, and talk about tech.
- **`#dsa-help`**: Stuck on a Data Structures or Algorithms problem? Paste your CodeTrace link here and the community will visually step through it with you.
- **`#web-dev`**: Discussions about React, Next.js, and browser APIs.
- **`#system-design`**: High-level architectural debates.

### ⚙️ CodeTrace Platform
- **`#announcements`**: The latest updates, new features, and changelogs.
- **`#bug-reports`**: Did the visualizer crash? Found an edge case in the V8 sandbox? Drop it here.
- **`#feature-requests`**: Tell us what language or feature you want to see visualized next.

### 🛠️ Open Source Development
- **`#engine-internals`**: Deep technical discussions about AST parsing and WebGL rendering.
- **`#pr-reviews`**: Need a maintainer to look at your Pull Request? Ping us here.

## 📜 Community Rules

To keep our environment welcoming and professional, we strictly enforce these rules:

1. **Be Respectful:** No harassment, sexism, racism, or hate speech. Zero tolerance.
2. **Help, Don't Handout:** If someone asks for help with code, guide them to the answer. Don't just paste the final solution.
3. **Keep it Clean:** No spam, self-promotion (outside of `#showcase`), or NSFW content.
4. **Use Threads:** For long debugging sessions, please create a thread in the help channels to avoid clutter.

## 🔒 Our Privacy Commitment

At CodeTrace, transparency is at our core. We believe that a developer tool should never act as spyware. This policy outlines exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

> *"We are building tools for developers, by developers. We only collect the bare minimum required to make your execution engine run blazing fast."*

---

## 📦 What We Collect

We keep our data footprint extremely small. Here is what we track:

| Data Type | Purpose | Retention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Information** | Authentication and securing your projects. | Until account deletion |
| **Execution Traces** | To render the visualizer and debug histories. | 7 Days (or immediately if manually cleared) |
| **Analytics (Opt-in)** | To understand performance bottlenecks in our engine. | Aggregated / Anonymized |

---

## 🚫 What We NEVER Collect

- **❌ Raw Source Code** (Unless you explicitly save it to your dashboard).
- **❌ Environment Variables** (All `.env` executions happen strictly in a volatile, isolated V8 sandbox).
- **❌ Third-party Tokens** (We do not scan or store any secrets passed into your code).

---

## 🛡️ How We Protect Your Data

CodeTrace uses industry-leading security practices:

1. **V8 Isolated Sandboxing:** Every code execution runs in a strictly isolated, ephemeral sandbox. The sandbox is destroyed the millisecond execution completes.
2. **End-to-End Encryption:** Data in transit is secured via TLS 1.3, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
3. **No Persistent Storage for Guests:** If you use CodeTrace without an account, your code is processed entirely in memory and never touches a database.

## 🤝 Third-Party Services

We use a select few third-party services to keep CodeTrace running smoothly:

- **Authentication:** Managed securely via OAuth and JWTs.
- **Hosting:** Deployed on enterprise-grade infrastructure.

*We do not, and will never, sell your data to third-party advertisers.*
